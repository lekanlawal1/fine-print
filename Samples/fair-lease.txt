RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

Landlord: Hillcrest Rentals Ltd.
Tenant: Alex Moreau
Rental unit: 27 Elm Avenue, Apartment 3, Kingston, Ontario

1. The Tenant will pay rent of $2,100 per month on the first day of each month.
2. The Tenant will pay a rent deposit of $2,100, which will be applied to the rent for the last month of the tenancy. The Landlord will pay interest on the deposit each year as required by law.
3. The Tenant may pay rent by cheque, e-transfer or pre-authorized debit, at the Tenant's choice.
4. The Landlord will provide two keys. A refundable key deposit of $40 is required, which is the cost of replacing both keys.
5. Except in an emergency, the Landlord will give the Tenant at least 24 hours' written notice before entering the unit, and will enter only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.
6. The rent will not be increased more than once in any 12-month period, and the Landlord will give at least 90 days' written notice of any increase.
7. The Tenant is responsible for obtaining tenant's insurance.
