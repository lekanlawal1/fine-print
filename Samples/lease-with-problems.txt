RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Maple Grove Properties Inc. (the "Landlord") and Jordan Ellis (the "Tenant") for Unit 4B, 112 King Street, Peterborough, Ontario.

1. TERM AND RENT
1.1 The tenancy begins on September 1, 2026 and continues for one year.
1.2 The Tenant shall pay rent of $1,800 per month, due on the first day of each month.

2. DEPOSITS
2.1 The Tenant shall pay a rent deposit of $1,800, to be applied to the last month of the tenancy.
2.2 The Tenant shall also pay a damage deposit of $600, refundable at the end of the tenancy if the unit is undamaged.

3. PAYMENT
3.1 The Tenant must provide twelve post-dated cheques for the year's rent before moving in.
3.2 A fee of $50 will be charged for any cheque returned NSF.
3.3 If the Tenant fails to pay rent when due, the rent for the remainder of the term becomes due immediately.

4. USE OF THE UNIT
4.1 No pets of any kind are permitted in the unit or the building.
4.2 Guests may not stay overnight for more than three nights in any month without the Landlord's written approval.

5. ACCESS
5.1 The Landlord may enter the unit at any time without notice to carry out inspections or repairs.

6. RENT INCREASES
6.1 The Landlord may increase the rent once every six months by giving the Tenant 60 days' written notice.
