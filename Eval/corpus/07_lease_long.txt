RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

BETWEEN: Northshore Living Inc. (the "Landlord")
AND: Taylor Brooks and Jamie Okoye (together, the "Tenant")
PREMISES: Suite 1407, 25 George Street, Toronto, Ontario

ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, "Premises" means the residential unit described above, together with any storage locker and parking space assigned to the Tenant. "Building" means the residential complex in which the Premises are located, including all common areas.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. Words in the singular include the plural and the reverse. Where there is more than one Tenant, their obligations are joint and several.

1.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

ARTICLE 2 - TERM

2.1 The tenancy begins on December 1, 2026 and ends on November 30, 2027. If neither party gives proper notice, the tenancy continues on a month-to-month basis as permitted by law.

2.2 The Tenant shall give the Landlord at least sixty days' written notice before the end of the term if the Tenant intends to vacate the Premises.

ARTICLE 3 - RENT

3.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of $2,450 per month, in advance, on the first day of each month.

3.2 Rent includes heat and water. The Tenant is responsible for electricity, internet and tenant insurance.

3.3 Rent shall be paid by pre-authorized debit from the Tenant's bank account, and the Tenant must sign the debit authorization before receiving keys.

3.4 An administration fee of $35 will be charged for any payment returned for insufficient funds.

3.5 The Landlord may increase the rent once in any twelve-month period by giving the Tenant at least 90 days' written notice.

ARTICLE 4 - DEPOSITS

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a rent deposit of $2,450, to be applied to the rent for the last month of the tenancy.

4.2 The Tenant shall pay a refundable deposit of $150 for two key fobs, which is the Landlord's cost of replacing them.

4.3 The Tenant shall also pay a cleaning deposit of $400, which will be returned at the end of the tenancy if the Premises are left in a clean condition.

ARTICLE 5 - USE AND OCCUPANCY

5.1 The Premises shall be used only as a private residence for the Tenant and the Tenant's immediate family.

5.2 The Tenant shall not carry on any business in the Premises that brings clients or customers to the Building.

5.3 Smoking of any substance is not permitted in the Premises, on the balcony or in the common areas.

5.4 The Tenant shall not keep any dogs, cats or other animals in the Premises.

5.5 The Tenant shall not install a satellite dish, window air conditioner or any fixture on the exterior of the Building without the Landlord's written consent.

ARTICLE 6 - MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

6.1 The Landlord shall keep the Premises and the Building in a good state of repair and fit for habitation, and shall comply with health, safety and housing standards.

6.2 The Tenant shall keep the Premises reasonably clean and shall promptly report any damage, leak or malfunction to the Landlord.

6.3 The Tenant shall be responsible for the cost of repairing any damage to the Premises caused by the wilful or negligent conduct of the Tenant or the Tenant's guests.

6.4 The Landlord may enter the Premises at any reasonable time to carry out repairs or inspections, provided that the Landlord gives the Tenant at least 6 hours' notice.

ARTICLE 7 - LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

7.1 The Tenant shall maintain tenant's insurance covering the Tenant's contents and personal liability, and shall provide proof of coverage on request.

7.2 The Landlord shall not be liable for any loss of or damage to the Tenant's property, howsoever caused, including loss caused by water, fire or theft.

7.3 The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against all claims, costs and legal fees arising from the Tenant's use of the Premises.

ARTICLE 8 - DEFAULT

8.1 If the Tenant fails to pay rent within five days of the due date, the full amount of rent for the balance of the term shall immediately become due and payable.

8.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits the rights of either party under the Residential Tenancies Act, 2006.

ARTICLE 9 - GENERAL

9.1 The Landlord may assign this Agreement to a purchaser of the Building without the Tenant's consent.

9.2 This Agreement, together with any rules of the Building provided in writing, is the entire agreement between the parties.

9.3 Notices to the Tenant may be delivered to the Premises. Notices to the Landlord must be delivered to the management office in the Building.
