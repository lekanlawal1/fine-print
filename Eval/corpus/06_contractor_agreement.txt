INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Brightline Media Inc. (the "Client") and Sam Okafor (the "Contractor").

1. Services. The Contractor will provide video editing services as described in each statement of work. The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Client.
2. Fees. The Client will pay the Contractor $55 per hour, invoiced monthly.
3. Non-Competition. During this agreement and for 6 months afterward, the Contractor will not provide editing services to any direct competitor of the Client.
4. Indemnity. The Contractor will indemnify the Client against any claims arising from the Contractor's work.
5. Termination. Either party may end this agreement with 14 days' written notice.
6. Ownership. The Contractor assigns to the Client all rights in the work product created under this agreement.
